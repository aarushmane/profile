Nemo earned **3rd Overall Best Project** and **2nd in the ASUS “Save the Earth” Sustainability Track** at HackHarvard Undergraduate Hackathon, October 16-18, 2026.

My contributions included conceiving the Grok-assisted physics workflow and writing simulation instructions exploring advection, diffusion, RK4, and Kalman filtering; developing export-validation and SHA-256 integrity checks; and helping design reproducible evaluation protocols. I also pitched Nemo to judges and sponsors, including Capital One and startup representatives.
