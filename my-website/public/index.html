03 / Research

A published study built from simulation and testing.

research.md

IEEE MIT Undergraduate Research Technology Conference | Presented October 2025

PID Control Optimization and Trajectory Tracking for Autonomous Differential Drive Robots

I co-authored a non-linear PID controller with moving integral windows, velocity-dependent target yaws, and anti-windup limits. We compared A* and Dijkstra path generation, modeled LiDAR obstacle avoidance in ROS GazeboSim, and tested the controller with TurtleBot Vicon tracking and matched-pairs tests at p < 0.01.
