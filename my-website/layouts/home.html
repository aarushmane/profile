Research

IEEE MIT URTC
October 2025

Co-author

PID control optimization & trajectory tracking for autonomous robots ↗

I co-authored a nonlinear PID controller with moving integral windows, velocity-dependent target yaws, and anti-windup limits. We compared A* and Dijkstra paths, modeled LiDAR obstacle avoidance in ROS GazeboSim, and tested the controller using TurtleBot Vicon tracking.

Controller performance evaluated with matched-pairs tests at p < 0.01.
